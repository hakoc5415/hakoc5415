THE SHORT VERSION

RO! has no login and no personal accounts. The only thing tied to you is an in-game player profile (a display name, a country and your best scores) stored on the World leaderboard against an anonymous device ID. You can delete it yourself from inside the game at any time — instantly, with no request needed.

1. DELETE IN THE GAME (INSTANT)
  1. Open RO! — Viking Raid.
  2. On the main menu, tap your player name at the top to open the Account screen.
  3. Select your profile and choose Delete.
This immediately removes your display name, country and scores from the World leaderboard, and removes the profile from your device. Uninstalling the app also erases all data stored locally on your device.
2. WHAT GETS DELETED
The player profile you created: the display name you chose, the country you selected, your best scores (per stage, best combo and best clear time), and the anonymous device ID the profile was stored under. No email address, phone number, real name or contact information is ever collected, so there is none to delete.
3. REQUEST DELETION BY EMAIL
If you can no longer open the app (for example you changed devices) and want your leaderboard entry removed, email norientinndev@gmail.com from any address with the player name and country you used. We will locate and delete that entry within 30 days.
4. RETENTION
Once deleted, your individual entry is removed and not retained. Aggregate, non-identifying country totals on the leaderboard may continue to reflect past play but never identify you.
5. CONTACT
Questions about your data: norientinndev@gmail.com · Norient Innovation and Development AS.
