OUR STORY

NORIENT brings together experienced professionals who have spent years delivering impactful projects across Europe. Our team carries deep expertise in EU-funded programs, educational training, and international cooperation.

The name "Norient" reflects our identity: Nordic values meeting global perspectives. We combine Scandinavian principles of quality, transparency, and social responsibility with an international outlook shaped by years of cross-border collaboration.

We are based in Norway and work across Europe — helping organizations access funding, design training programs, and build lasting partnerships through Erasmus+ and other EU initiatives.
